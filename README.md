# 🎨 Creature Inspiration Generator | 奇幻生物灵感生成器

An interactive web-based inspiration generator for artists, designers, and creature conceptualizers. Easily create unique creature concepts by randomly pairing 9 distinct body parts with over 1,000+ real animals, insects, mythical beasts, and prehistoric dinosaurs.

专为画家、概念设计师及奇幻生物爱好者打造的网页版灵感生成器。通过为 9 个不同部位随机分配物种，轻松碰撞出全新的生物设想。内置 1,000+ 种真实动物、昆虫、神话神兽及恐龙古生物库。

---

## ✨ Features / 核心功能

- 🐾 **1000+ Species Database / 庞大物种库**  
  Includes real-world animals, insects, Eastern & Western mythic beasts, and prehistoric dinosaurs (with bilingual Chinese & English names).  
  涵盖真实动物、昆虫、东西方神话神兽及古生物恐龙（中英对照）。

- 🧩 **9 Anatomy Parts / 9 大部位独立组合**  
  Base, Head, Ears, Eyes, Nose, Legs, Feets, Tail, Coat.  
  基础、头部、耳朵、眼睛、鼻子、腿、脚掌、尾巴、外皮。

- 🎲 **Flexible Generation / 灵活生成方式**  
  Click individual parts to unlock step-by-step or use **Roll All** for instant randomized combinations.  
  支持逐个点击解锁局部灵感，也支持一键随机生成全身组合。

- 📷 **One-Click Export / 一键导出卡片**  
  Save your generated inspiration card as a high-resolution PNG picture for quick drawing reference.  
  支持一键将生成结果导出为高清 PNG 图片卡片，方便保存至本地作为创作参考。
